# 📊 AcadEval+ Simple Step-by-Step Scoring Guide

This guide explains **exactly how your final project score (out of 100)** is calculated in simple arithmetic terms without complex mathematical symbols.

---

## 🏆 1. How the Overall Total Score is Calculated

Your final **Total Score (out of 100)** is the sum of **7 dimension scores**, each multiplied by its assigned weight percentage:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Total Score} &= (\text{Novelty Score} \times 0.20) \\
&+ (\text{Technical Depth Score} \times 0.20) \\
&+ (\text{Feasibility Score} \times 0.15) \\
&+ (\text{Completeness Score} \times 0.15) \\
&+ (\text{Clarity Score} \times 0.10) \\
&+ (\text{Publication Potential Score} \times 0.10) \\
&+ ((100 - \text{Similarity Risk \%}) \times 0.10)
\end{aligned}$$

## 🧮 3. Worked Numerical Example

Suppose your project gets the following scores:
- **Novelty**: `61.9 / 100`
- **Technical Depth**: `82.0 / 100`
- **Feasibility**: `80.0 / 100`
- **Completeness**: `70.0 / 100`
- **Clarity**: `80.0 / 100`
- **Publication Potential**: `85.0 / 100`
- **Similarity Risk**: `15%` (Originality = `100 - 15 = 85.0`)

### Simple Addition Step:
1. **Novelty Contribution**: $61.9 \times 0.20 = \mathbf{12.38}$ points
2. **Technical Depth Contribution**: $82.0 \times 0.20 = \mathbf{16.40}$ points
3. **Feasibility Contribution**: $80.0 \times 0.15 = \mathbf{12.00}$ points
4. **Completeness Contribution**: $70.0 \times 0.15 = \mathbf{10.50}$ points
5. **Clarity Contribution**: $80.0 \times 0.10 = \mathbf{8.00}$ points
6. **Publication Potential Contribution**: $85.0 \times 0.10 = \mathbf{8.50}$ points
7. **Originality Contribution**: $(100 - 15) \times 0.10 = \mathbf{8.50}$ points

### Final Sum:
$$\text{Final Total Score} = 12.38 + 16.40 + 12.00 + 10.50 + 8.00 + 8.50 + 8.50 = \mathbf{76.28 / 100}$$

## 🏷️ 4. Grade Scale Matrix

- **90.0 to 100.0** ➔ **Grade A+** (Exceptional Innovation)
- **80.0 to 89.9** ➔ **Grade A** (High Quality Work)
- **70.0 to 79.9** ➔ **Grade B** (Good Technical Foundation)
- **Below 70.0** ➔ **Grade C / Fail** (Needs Structural Revisions)

---

## ✏️ 5. How Faculty / Guides Can Modify Any Score
If your guide wants to adjust your final score:
1. They click **"Override Grade / Scores"** in the web report.
2. They enter a new score for any dimension (e.g. increase Novelty from `61.9` to `85.0`).
3. They write a reason comment and click **"Save Faculty Score"**.
4. The system automatically recalculates the total score using the exact table above!
